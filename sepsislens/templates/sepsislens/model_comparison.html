Primary Finding
Why Random Forest Was Selected — And Its Clinical Trade-offs

In the notebook's honest patient-level split, Random Forest was selected primarily because it achieved the highest ROC-AUC (0.7834) among all models evaluated without SMOTE. However, its superiority across all clinical metrics is not established:

  • Precision & PR-AUC: Logistic Regression achieved higher Precision (0.1221 vs 0.0921) and higher PR-AUC (0.0960 vs 0.0601), generating fewer false alarms per true case.
  • Sensitivity / Recall: Decision Tree achieved much higher Recall (0.3822 vs 0.2000), capturing almost double the proportion of sepsis cases at its tuned threshold, though with higher false positives.
  • F1 Score: Logistic Regression (0.1503), Linear SVM (0.1498), and Decision Tree (0.1456) all achieved higher F1 scores than Random Forest (0.1261) at their respective validation-tuned thresholds.
  • Conclusion: Model selection in clinical sepsis depends on the operational tolerance for alert fatigue. Random Forest was selected as the default deployment candidate for its superior threshold-independent discrimination (ROC-AUC), robust non-linear handling, and stability under missing data, but is not universally optimal for every clinical objective.
Extreme Class Imbalance Context
With only 1.52% sepsis prevalence in the test cohort (1 septic hour for every 65 normal hours), raw accuracy (95.8%) is misleading.
A naive model predicting "no sepsis" always achieves 98.5% accuracy while catching zero patients. Therefore, ROC-AUC and PR-AUC were the notebook's primary decision drivers.

Methodological Integrity: Preventing The "Paper-Style" Leakage Fallacy

Cell 31 of ML_Project.ipynb demonstrates a common flaw in published ML literature: randomly shuffling rows across all patients and applying SMOTE before splitting:

Flawed Protocol (Row Shuffle + Pre-Split SMOTE)
Accuracy: 0.9987 | F1: 0.9987 | ROC-AUC: 0.9999
Artificially inflated metrics caused by rows from the same patient appearing in both train and test splits, leaking synthetic patient data.
Honest Protocol (Patient-Level Stratified Split)
Accuracy: 0.9578 | F1: 0.1261 | ROC-AUC: 0.7834
Strict patient grouping ensures zero patient overlap between train, validation, and test cohorts. SepsisLens strictly preserves this honest protocol.
