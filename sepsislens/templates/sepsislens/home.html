Exact Reproduction from Notebook

Raw ICU observations suffer from extreme sparsity (>90% missingness across key labs). Rather than discarding sparse tests or leaking future information, SepsisLens reproduces the notebook's exact multi-channel feature expansion:

1. Static & Missingness (32)
6 static demographic features (Age, Gender, Unit, HospAdmTime, ICULOS) + 26 missingness indicator flags (_msg) recording clinical ordering patterns.
2. Forward-Fill & Sampling (48)
34 vital and lab signals forward-filled within patient stays + 14 cumulative count features (_cnt) measuring observation frequency.
3. Trends & Dynamics (84)
56 rolling statistics (mean, min, max, std over 6h) + 14 hour-to-hour differentials (_d1) + 14 baseline drift vectors (_dbase).
